# 02 · 引言：从问题走到目的
<!-- test: tool=lint section=introduction bad=I1 good_no=I1 -->

任务：让读者接受“这个问题值得问”，并知道本文要回答它的哪一部分。

## 坏例

```bad
Depression is a major global health problem that affects millions of people and imposes a substantial burden on society. A growing body of literature has investigated depressive symptoms in adolescents. Smith et al. examined symptom severity. Jones et al. examined sleep. Lee et al. examined school environments. Network analysis is a powerful approach that conceptualizes mental disorders as systems of interacting symptoms, and it has been widely applied in recent years. This is the first study to fill the gap in the literature on boarding schools.
```

## 为什么坏

1. **文献罗列。** 三句“某某研究了某某”，读者只知道有人做过，不知道已经知道了什么。
2. **没有张力。** 缺口应当从“已有解释解释不了什么”长出来，而不是从“没人做过”长出来。
3. **方法教程。** “网络分析是一种强大的方法”属于方法节；引言里只需一句说明这个方法为什么能回答这个问题。
4. **“first study to fill the gap”没有检索依据。** 新颖性要写窄到能被核实。
5. **缺目的句。** 读完不知道本文到底问什么。引言最后两段必须出现研究问题、目的或假设。

## 改写

```good
Depressive symptoms in adolescence rarely occur alone. Poor sleep, low mood and loss of interest tend to appear together, and several accounts hold that they sustain one another rather than all following from one underlying cause. Studies that compare groups have mostly asked whether symptoms are more severe in one group than another, which leaves open a different question: whether the symptoms hang together differently.

Living arrangements are one place where this could matter. Boarding pupils spend weekdays away from family, follow a shared timetable and sleep in dormitories, conditions that have been linked to poorer sleep and to more depressive symptoms. If these shared conditions act on several symptoms at once, the symptoms should be more tightly connected in boarding pupils even where average severity is similar.

This study therefore compared depressive-symptom networks in boarding and day pupils from six secondary schools. Two questions were specified before analysis: whether the pattern of connections among symptoms differs between school types, and whether the overall strength of connections differs. The analysis also identified which symptoms were most connected with the others in each group.
```

三段分别承担：问题与已有解释 → 具体张力和可检验的预期 → 本文怎样回答。缺口写成“已有研究问的是另一个问题”，可以核实；不写“首次”。

## 这份范例不校准什么

- 引言长度。同类文献差别很大（英文临床心理领域包中位约 800 词，范围 141–2916）。
- 综合高影响刊（Nature 系、eLife、PNAS）的引言末尾常预告结果，这是那一类期刊的惯例；多数学科期刊不预告，引言以目的与假设收尾。先看目标刊与类型匹配的近邻，目标刊未定时不预告。
