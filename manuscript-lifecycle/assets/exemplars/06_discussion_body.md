# 06 · 讨论主体：解释，而不是复述
<!-- test: tool=lint section=discussion bad=D1 good_no=D1 -->

任务：把发现接到解释、可比证据和含义上；清楚区分数据支持的和推测的。

## 坏例

```bad
Our finding that global strength was 3.92 in boarding pupils and 3.41 in day pupils is consistent with previous research. Smith et al. also found differences between groups. This is an important finding that advances our understanding of adolescent depression. The finding that sleep problems had the highest expected influence (1.21) is in line with the literature showing that sleep is important. Future research should examine other populations with larger samples and longitudinal designs.
```

## 为什么坏

1. **复述数字。** 三个数字在结果节已经给过，这里重复出现，却没有带来新的判断。
2. **“consistent with previous research”没说与谁一致、比的是不是同一个量。** 别人比较的是症状严重度，本文比较的是连接强度，不是一回事。
3. **自我表扬。** “This is an important finding”应当由内容体现。
4. **只有一种解释。** 没提替代解释，也没说数据更支持哪一个。
5. **未来研究写成了套话。** “更大的样本、纵向设计”适用于任何论文。

## 改写

```good
Why are symptoms more tightly connected in boarding pupils? Shared conditions, such as a fixed timetable, dormitory sleep and less daily contact with family, can affect several symptoms at once and so raise their co-occurrence. Alternatively, tighter connections may mark a group with more severe symptoms, because networks estimated in more symptomatic samples show stronger connections. These two accounts make different predictions: under the first, the difference should persist after matching on symptom severity; under the second, it should not. Whether severity differs between the two school types should therefore be checked before the difference is attributed to boarding itself.

Expected influence describes how strongly a symptom co-occurs with others at one time point, so it does not show that sleep problems produce the other symptoms or that treating sleep would reduce them. The prominence of sleep problems nonetheless makes it a natural focus for studies that follow pupils over time, where the order in which symptoms change can be observed.
```

两段分别承担：两个竞争解释以及区分它们的办法；一个容易被过度解读的指标该读到哪一步。没有重复结果数字，每一步都说得出根据。

## 深度阶梯（缺的步骤是补写的候选，不是必填模板）

| 步 | 问题 |
|---|---|
| 1 意味着什么 | 用读者的话，这个发现怎么说？ |
| 2 与谁比 | 和谁一致、和谁不同？比的是同一个量吗？ |
| 3 为什么 | 有哪些解释？数据更支持哪一个？怎样把它们区分开？ |
| 4 意义 | 对理论或实践意味着什么？ |
| 5 边界 | 在什么条件下不成立？ |
| 6 下一步 | 什么样的设计能回答剩下的问题？ |

只补本文问题需要、并且有证据支持的那几步。没有可比证据就说没有；没有临床用途就不写临床段落。宁可短。

## 这份范例不校准什么

- 每条发现是否都要写满一段。次要发现可以合并。
- 讨论里出现一两个数字不是错误；复述整张表才是。
