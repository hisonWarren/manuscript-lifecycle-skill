# 08 · 结论：短，并且说清带来什么
<!-- test: tool=lint section=conclusion bad=C2,P1 good_no=C2,P1 -->

任务：回答研究问题，说明适用范围。读者能用一句话向别人复述这篇论文的贡献。

## 坏例

```bad
In conclusion, the present study investigated depressive symptom networks in boarding and day school pupils using state-of-the-art network analysis methods. We found that the network structure was invariant across groups (M = 0.14, p = .41) while global strength differed significantly (3.92 vs 3.41, p = .02), and that sleep problems had the highest expected influence in both groups (1.21 and 1.15). Three edges differed between groups. These findings have important implications for theory and practice. Schools should implement sleep-focused interventions for boarding students, and policymakers should reconsider boarding arrangements. Further research is warranted to confirm these findings in other populations.
```

## 为什么坏

1. **复述全文。** 方法、四个统计量、三条边又出现了一遍，读者读到的是摘要的第二版。
2. **口号。** state-of-the-art、important implications for theory and practice 不携带信息。
3. **建议越过了证据。** 没有检验过任何干预，却写“学校应当……”“政策制定者应当……”。
4. **新增了推断。** 结论不是引入新判断的地方。
5. **“Further research is warranted”是套话**，没说清要回答哪个剩余问题。

## 改写

```good
In boarding and day pupils the same depressive symptoms were linked to one another, but the links were tighter in boarding pupils. The difference is therefore one of degree rather than of kind, and it concerns how symptoms co-occur at one point in time, not which symptoms cause which. Whether tighter connections precede more persistent symptoms is the question that follow-up data would answer.
```

三句话：回答、适用范围、剩下的问题。没有统计量，没有建议，没有新证据。

## 这份范例不校准什么

- 结论的长度上限因刊而异；本领域已发表论文的分布见领域包。中文稿的结论通常比英文长，有些期刊还要求分条写。
- 结论里出现术语、数字或引文，如果确有必要而且期刊允许，可以保留。
